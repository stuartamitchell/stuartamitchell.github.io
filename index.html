Stuart Mitchell

PhD student in mathematics
stuart.mitchell [at] anu [dot] edu [dot] au

I am a PhD student in the Mathematical Sciences Institute at the Australian National University. My research interests include:

  • Categorification: Heisenberg categories and their generalisations, diagrammatic (string-diagram) calculi, and what their centres and Grothendieck rings tell us.
  • Representation theory: degenerate affine Hecke algebras, induction and restriction functors for towers of algebras, and the combinatorics that comes with them.
  • Algebraic combinatorics: shifted symmetric functions and their connections to free probability and asymptotic representation theory.

My current work builds on my earlier paper with Henry Kvinge and Anthony Licata, which connected Khovanov's Heisenberg category with free probability and shifted symmetric functions.

Alongside research I have taught secondary mathematics, and I am interested in university-level teaching.
